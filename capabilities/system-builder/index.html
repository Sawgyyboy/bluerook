The design contract

02

Tools are replaceable. Operating obligations are not.

State

One accountable record

Every signal resolves to a known state instead of disappearing between inboxes, tabs and people.

Ownership

Every next action belongs somewhere

Automation carries routine movement; responsibility never becomes anonymous.

Control

Human boundaries are designed

Approval, sensitive judgement, exceptions and irreversible actions stop at a visible gate.

Completion

Done is a state, not activity

Success means the owner, outcome and next action are legible—not merely that a workflow ran.

Failure

Recovery is part of the system

Retries, queues, alerts and manual routes must preserve context and avoid silent duplication.

Evidence

Claims inherit source status

Implemented, product, prototype and fictional-demonstration labels remain explicit at the point of use.
