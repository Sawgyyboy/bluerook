01 · One call, end to end

After hours.
Still answered.

  1. The phone rings at 21:47

    The team is offline. Arden answers inside approved coverage: known questions, prepared availability, structured capture. Everything else waits for a person, and says so.

  2. An approved answer, not an improvised one

    The question is classified before it is answered. Arden replies only from approved knowledge, so it cannot invent a price, a promise or a policy at midnight.

  3. A booking is prepared, not assumed

    Two real slots are offered and a hold is prepared. Nothing lands on the calendar until the caller explicitly confirms, because a wrong booking costs more than no booking.

  4. A person is one sentence away

    The moment the caller asks, automation stops. The handoff carries the reason, the context and the contact preference, so nobody repeats themselves tomorrow.

03 · When Arden stops

The handoff lands
somewhere visible.

A handoff is not a transferred call ringing into the void. The bot goes quiet on that one thread, the conversation joins a queue a person actually watches, and the bot resumes only when the person is done. Take a thread over yourself.
