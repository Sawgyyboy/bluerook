# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders and high-performing entrepreneurs who have become the operational
bottleneck in their business. They are evaluating whether Bluerook can take
ownership of recurring workflows and operational systems without creating more
management overhead.

## Product Purpose

The Bluerook marketing site explains the agency's operating model, demonstrates
the systems it can design and run, and converts qualified visitors into a free
30-minute strategy call.

## Positioning

Bluerook sells operational ownership rather than cheap hours, generic
automation, or standalone dashboards. Its AI System VAs are role-based operating
systems with defined workflows, permissions, human approval points, exception
paths, and reporting.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive from referrals, LinkedIn, direct outreach, or a live AI voice
agent demonstration. They need to understand concrete system patterns such as
inbound reception, speed to lead, lead activation, CRM operations, website
support, executive operations, and decision dashboards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site is a single-page vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript application
  with no build step. One Vercel function securely creates Retell web-call tokens.
- The primary conversion is a free 30-minute strategy call.
- The site may show illustrative system blueprints, but must not present them as
  deployed client systems or invent results.
- AI System VAs are software systems, never human virtual assistants.
- The broad founder audience remains canonical. Youth sports and other niche
  offers remain unconfirmed hypotheses.
- The canonical domain is `https://bluerook.co`.

## Brand Commitments

Bluerook is one word with a capital B. Preserve the exact approved rook and the
Midnight / Paper / Bluerook Blue / Brass system. Display accents use Cormorant
Garamond Italic; body and interface copy use Geist; labels and metadata use
Geist Mono. The voice is editorial, confident, restrained, and operationally
precise. The primary slogan is “We run your operations. You run your empire.”

## Product Principles

1. Make the operating system visible, not merely described.
2. Explain ownership, controls, approvals, and exception handling.
3. Demonstrate concrete workflows without pretending illustrative data is live.
4. Keep the primary path from recognition to strategy-call booking direct.
5. Make premium execution feel calm, controlled, and specific.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts,
and reduced-motion preferences. Motion may enrich the narrative but may not be
required to understand or use the page.
