01 · Order operations

One late parcel.
Zero wrong promises.

  1. An order leaves the building

    Customer view, support macro and dashboard all report the same approved state. That agreement is the product, and most stacks lose it the moment anything goes wrong.

  2. The carrier misses a day

    The exception pauses automation instead of guessing. The customer promise freezes rather than lies, because a wrong apology is worse than a slow one.

  3. A person owns the promise

    A named human decides the revised delivery and the apology. One approval fans the update out to every surface at once.

  4. The outcome is written down

    Delivered as revised. Exception, decision, owner and outcome land in the audit line — the deliverable, not a side effect.
