01 · Intake to handoff

An enquiry at 21:40.
Nobody chases it.

  1. It arrives after hours

    From a phone, half the details missing. On most setups this waits in an inbox until somebody opens it tomorrow morning, and the parent has already messaged two other academies.

  2. It qualifies itself against real constraints

    Age band, programme, location and schedule are checked against actual capacity, not a generic autoresponder. The reply is specific because the system knows what is actually available.

  3. A trial is held and the record is written

    The slot decrements, the CRM record carries an owner and a next action, and the confirmation is queued. Three systems changed; the parent typed one sentence.

  4. Judgement stops the machine

    A payment question is not an automation decision. The sequence halts, a named person inherits the full conversation, and the booking is preserved either way.

02 · One record, every channel

The conversation becomes
a record you can trust.

  1. Every channel lands on one board

    WhatsApp, Instagram, forms and calls create cards in the same tracker, deduplicated by phone number. There is no second list to reconcile on Friday.

  2. The agent fills the record, not a form

    Sport, age band, region, date of birth, lead score: extracted from the conversation and written to the card. Click any lead on the board to read what was collected.

  3. Handoff is a state, not a hope

    One field flips to human_control and the bot goes quiet on this thread only. Staff see exactly why, with the full message history attached. Every other lead keeps moving.

  4. Resolved, and the bot resumes

    The staff outcome is logged on the same record, control returns to the bot, and the card moves on. One record, zero retyping, nothing lost between tools.
