# Public claim ledger

## Approved portfolio-wide claims

- Bluerook designs and runs role-based AI operating systems and connected workflows with explicit permissions, human controls, exception paths and reporting.
- Bluerook sells operational ownership, visible handoffs, responsiveness and completed outcomes rather than disconnected tools.
- Public portfolio interactions use synthetic or safely reconstructed data and do not call production customer systems.
- Every project surface identifies whether it is an anonymized implementation, Bluerook product, fictional demonstration, capability prototype, available capability or managed service.
- Human review remains visible around judgement, relationships, sensitive context, high-value decisions and irreversible actions.

## Claims excluded everywhere

- Client names, endorsements, testimonials or permission assumptions.
- Revenue, conversion, time-saved, accuracy, user-count, lead-volume or performance results.
- Live, deployed or production-ready status not verified in current source.
- Real contacts, records, internal domains, workflow/database identifiers or prompt text.
- Approved pricing or fixed hourly rates.
- Youth sports as confirmed Bluerook specialization; it remains a working hypothesis.
