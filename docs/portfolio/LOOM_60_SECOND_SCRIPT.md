# 60-second Loom script

## Purpose

A concise first-touch walkthrough for a technical opportunity or operations-system conversation.

## Recording path

`/work/` → `/work/follow-up-gap-detector/` → `/technical-portfolio/` in Summary mode.

## Script

**0:00–0:10 — Portfolio lobby**

“Hi, I’m Hatim Beid, founder of Bluerook. I build the operation behind the interface: the path from a signal to an owner, a next action and a visible completion condition.”

**0:10–0:27 — Operations board**

“This portfolio makes that path interactive. The commerce and sports scenes are clearly labelled fictional demonstrations, while products, capability prototypes and managed services keep their own evidence boundaries beside the work.”

**0:27–0:45 — Follow-Up Gap Detector**

“Here, the Follow-Up Gap Detector scans six synthetic CRM records. It flags five deterministic ownership or next-action gaps, excludes the healthy record and prepares context for human review. The workflow is inactive and sends no outreach.”

**0:45–1:00 — Technical summary**

“The technical portfolio shows the same approach across interfaces, workflow packages and browser-local prototypes: model the state, expose failure, preserve human judgement and verify the handoff. If that is useful for your team, I’d welcome a conversation.”

## Recording guardrails

- Keep the status labels in frame when describing a project.
- Do not click booking, email or other external links during the recording.
- Do not describe any scene as a live client deployment.
