- Historical generated metadata without current source.
- Raw workflow exports, IDs, prompts, internal URLs and customer records.
- Reference branding or screenshots without permission.
- Fixed synthetic KPIs presented as real outcomes.
- Claims of production state inferred from local prototypes.
