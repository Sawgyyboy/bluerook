# Loom recording checklist

## Choose the cut

- Use the 60-second script for a first touch.
- Use the 90-second script when technical judgement and evidence boundaries matter.
- Use the 2-minute script when the recipient expects an ecosystem overview.
- Record one clean version. Do not splice in live dashboards, customer systems or reference-workspace footage.

## Prepare the local site

- Start the preview from `C:\Users\ROG\Bluerook` with `python -m http.server 5173 --bind 127.0.0.1`.
- Open the required routes on `http://localhost:5173` before recording.
- Use a clean browser profile or window with no private bookmarks, extensions, notifications or unrelated tabs visible.
- Reset every demonstration before the take.
- Set the intended mode explicitly: Story for guided pacing, Inspect for evidence, Summary for the close.
- Confirm Pause and Motion controls are in their intended states.
- Keep browser zoom at 100% unless a route-specific visual review requires otherwise.

## Public-safety check

- Show status labels beside the work; do not crop them out.
- Use only the synthetic records already supplied by the portfolio.
- Do not show reference workspaces, source paths, `.env` files, terminals containing identifiers, agent prompts or private archives.
- Do not add client names, testimonials, revenue, conversion, time-saved, accuracy, usage or production claims.
- Do not call Sports Operations OS a live system or sports academies a confirmed Bluerook specialization.
- Describe commerce as a fictional capability reconstruction; the available source verifies only a storefront prototype.
- Describe workflow packages as inactive and credential-free. Meeting Recap delivery and Follow-Up Gap Detector outreach remain disabled.
- Describe Process to SOP as in local validation; its portfolio preview calls no live AI service.
- Describe Arden as a local prepared-scenario prototype. It places no call and connects to no customer system.
- Do not enable the microphone meter during outreach recording. If it is ever demonstrated, obtain browser permission deliberately and repeat that audio stays local, is not recorded and is not transmitted.

## Interaction rehearsal

- Verify every route loads locally before recording.
- Rehearse the exact buttons used in the selected script.
- Keep one success path and one human-control boundary in the take; avoid racing through every control.
- Let state changes finish before moving the cursor.
- Use keyboard focus only if it is part of the story and the focus ring is visible in the capture.
- Avoid clicking email, booking or other external links.
- Do not open a print dialog during the Loom unless the export is the subject of the recording.

## Picture and sound

- Record at 1440×900 or 1280×720 when possible.
- Keep the browser content centered and the mode dock unobstructed.
- Hide the operating-system taskbar if it contains unrelated applications or names.
- Use a neutral microphone input and disable system notifications.
- Read at a calm pace. Do not compress disclosure sentences or status labels.
- Use the exact Bluerook name: one word, capital B.

## Final review

- Watch the entire recording with sound before sharing.
- Confirm the spoken duration matches the selected script.
- Confirm no private data, identifiers, notifications or unintended tabs appear in any frame.
- Confirm every claim still matches `docs/portfolio/CLAIM_LEDGER.md`.
- Confirm the first ten seconds state the operating outcome and the final ten seconds contain one clear invitation to talk.
- Use a restrained title such as `Hatim Beid — Inspectable operations systems`.
- Do not upload or share until the portfolio’s route, visual, privacy and security reviews are complete.
