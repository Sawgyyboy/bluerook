## Customization rules

- Quote or paraphrase only responsibilities present in the role description.
- Do not invent familiarity with the company, team, product, users or technical stack.
- Do not state availability dates, compensation, location or work authorization unless confirmed separately.
- Do not call a fictional or prototype route production work.
- Prefer one relevant route over a long tool list.
