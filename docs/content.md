## Services — "Two operating models. One unfair advantage."

Eyebrow "What we do." Lede: *Recurring infrastructure to run the business you already

### Pillar I — Recurring Infrastructure (Vol. I · Continuous · Monthly retainer)
Always-on execution squads, fortnightly architectural reviews, and an isolated staging
environment. Three vectors:

- **I·01 Operational VAs** — Fractional or dedicated execution units tied to strict
  SLAs, trained on your SOPs, managed by an ops lead. (Dedicated & fractional tiers ·
  documented hand-offs · priority response lane.)
- **I·02 Social Ecosystems** — Channel management, not posts. Content pipeline,
  distribution, growth tracking, community ops. (Editorial calendar · long/short-form
  production · distribution + growth reporting.)
- **I·03 AI Maintenance & Optimization** — Continuous oversight of deployed
  automations; bug fixes, API updates, fortnightly audit. (Monitored 24/7, reviewed
  every 14 days · versioned changes · quarterly performance & cost report.)

### Pillar II — Managed Implementations (Vol. II · Bespoke · Milestone-based)
End-to-end engineering, each build sandboxed before production, documented as an asset
you keep. Two vectors:

- **II·04 Custom AI Builds** — End-to-end design, development, deployment of bespoke
  architectures. Milestone-priced. Yours from day it ships. (Discovery → architecture
  → build → handover · sandboxed · living documentation you keep forever.)
- **II·05 System Audits & Architecture** — Consulting phase mapping every bottleneck +
  the remediation blueprint. Standalone or first move of a build. (Operational map ·
  cost-per-hour analysis · pre-engineered remediation roadmap.)

**Whisper (fine print):** *Engagements move on a first-architect basis. Active rosters
reviewed quarterly — onboarding by referral or audit only.*

## How it works — "From bottleneck to back-of-house — in four moves."

1. **Discovery** — *Map your bottlenecks.* A working session to inventory every task
   draining you: what it costs per hour, where the leverage lives.
2. **System Design** — *Engineer the delegation stack.* VAs, automations, SOPs designed
   as one system; hand-offs documented; failure points removed before they exist.
3. **Deployment** — *Live in 48 hours.* VAs onboarded, automations switched on.
4. **Scale** — *Optimize as you grow.* Monthly reviews; capacity expands with you.

## Why Bluerook — "Four things we refuse to do."

- **No generic VAs.** Every assistant matched to your workflow, trained on your SOPs,
  managed by an ops lead who owns the outcome.
- **No off-the-shelf automations.** We build to your stack — no template-shaped holes.
- **No micromanagement.** We own the outcome; reports on your schedule, not ours.
- **No lock-in.** No annual contracts, no exit fees. We earn the next month every month.
