Privacy at Bluerook

Short version: this website stores nothing about you. The two things that do send data anywhere are the callback form and the chat, and both only run when you choose to use them.

We do not run analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this site. No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no session recording.

What happens when you browse

Nothing is collected. The site is static files served by Vercel, our hosting provider. Vercel keeps short-lived server logs that include IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. We do not read those logs to build a profile of you.

When you ask for a callback

If you enter a name and mobile number and press the button, that name and number are sent to Retell AI, the service that places the call, so the agent can dial you. We do not save them on our own servers and we do not add you to any list. There is a limit of one call per number per day.

Please only enter a number that belongs to you.

When you use the chat or the browser voice demo

Messages you type are sent to Anthropic to generate a reply. Microphone audio in the browser voice demo is sent to Retell AI for the length of the conversation. We do not store transcripts.

When you book a call

Booking goes to Google Calendar or Calendly. Whatever you enter there is held by that provider under their own terms, and by us as a record of the meeting.

Services we rely on

  • Vercel for hosting and server logs
  • Retell AI for outbound calls and browser voice
  • Anthropic for chat replies
  • Google for fonts and calendar bookings
  • Calendly as an alternative booking route

Google Fonts are requested from Google servers when a page loads, which discloses your IP address to Google.

Your rights

Because we hold almost nothing, there is usually nothing to export or erase. If you have emailed or booked with us and want that removed, write to hatim@bluerook.co and we will delete it.

Demonstration data

Every record, name, metric and dashboard figure shown anywhere on this site is invented for demonstration. None of it is a real customer.
