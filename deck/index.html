09 · Where it stops

A system is trusted for what it refuses to do.

It will not

Invent a price

Every figure comes from an approved list. No list, no number.

It will not

Publish unapproved

Edits are held at a named approver. An accidental keystroke never reaches a customer.

It will not

Guess at judgement

Refunds, disputes, payment arrangements exit to a person with full context.

It will not

Send on its own

The recovery scan reviews and reports. There is no send node in it.

Every exit above is designed and visible on the canvas. None of them is an error path.
